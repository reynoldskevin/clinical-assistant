## Warfarin INR Monitoring

<!-- context: From the Anticoagulation Management guideline; how often to check warfarin INR monitoring, the stable target range for atrial fibrillation, and when to adjust the dose. -->
Patients on warfarin should have their INR checked regularly until stable, then
at least every four weeks. The usual target INR range for atrial fibrillation is
2.0 to 3.0. An INR above the target range increases bleeding risk and may require
a dose reduction or a held dose.

## Drug Interactions

<!-- context: From the Anticoagulation Management guideline; which co-prescribed medications interact with this anticoagulant and how to review new prescriptions for interaction risk. -->
Warfarin interacts with many medications. Verapamil and amiodarone can raise the
INR by inhibiting warfarin metabolism, increasing bleeding risk. Review every new
prescription for interactions before starting it alongside warfarin.

## Bleeding Risk Assessment

<!-- context: From the Anticoagulation Management guideline; how to assess hemorrhage risk with a structured score and which factors warrant closer monitoring during therapy. -->
Assess bleeding risk before and during anticoagulation using a structured score.
Prior major bleeding, uncontrolled hypertension, and concurrent antiplatelet use
all raise the risk of hemorrhage and warrant closer monitoring.
