## Glycemic Targets

A reasonable HbA1c goal for many non-pregnant adults is below 7.0 percent. Less
stringent targets may suit patients with limited life expectancy or a history of
severe hypoglycemia. Individualize the target to the patient.

## HbA1c Monitoring

Measure HbA1c at least twice per year in patients meeting treatment goals, and
quarterly when therapy changes or glycemic control is not at target. HbA1c
reflects average glucose over the preceding two to three months.

## Metformin First-Line Therapy

Metformin is the preferred initial pharmacologic agent for type 2 diabetes unless
contraindicated. Start at a low dose with meals and titrate slowly to reduce
gastrointestinal side effects. Reassess renal function before and during therapy.
