## Blood Pressure Targets

A common office blood pressure target for adults with hypertension is below
130/80 mmHg. Confirm elevated readings with repeat measurements, and where
possible corroborate with home or ambulatory monitoring before escalating therapy.

## Lifestyle Modification

Recommend lifestyle changes for all patients with elevated blood pressure: reduced
sodium intake, regular physical activity, weight loss when overweight, and limiting
alcohol. These measures can meaningfully lower blood pressure on their own.

## Pharmacologic Treatment

First-line antihypertensive options include thiazide diuretics, ACE inhibitors,
angiotensin receptor blockers, and calcium channel blockers. Choose the agent
based on comorbidities, and add a second agent when a single drug does not reach
the target.
